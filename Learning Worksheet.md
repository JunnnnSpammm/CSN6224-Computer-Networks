## Chapter 1

1. In a data communication system, the five components are ***sender***, ***message***, receiver, transmission medium, and protocol.
2. The three key characteristics that determine communication effectiveness are ***delivery***, ***accuracy***, and timeliness.
3. The three data flow types are ***simplex***, half-duplex, and full-duplex.
4. In a network topology, the four basic types are ***mesh***, ***star***, bus, and ring.
5. A network must meet three key criteria: performance, ***reliabilty***, and ***security***.
6. The three categories of networks based on size and coverage are ***LAN***, ***MAN***, and ***WAN***.
7. In the OSI model, there are ***7*** layers, while in the TCP/IP model, there are typically ***5*** layers.
8. A protocol defines the rules of communication, including syntax, semantics, and ***timing***.
9. The two types of standards are ***de facto*** (adopted by common use) and ***de jure*** (approved by official bodies).
10. In encapsulation, data at the transport layer is called a ***segment (TCP) or user datagram (UDP)***, at the network layer a ***datagram***, and at the data link layer a ***frame***.
